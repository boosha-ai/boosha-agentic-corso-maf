# The Last Airbender (2010)
*Regia di M. Night Shyamalan*

- **Incasso**: $319.7 million
- **Rotten Tomatoes**: 5%

## Trama
The Last Airbender is a 2010 American fantasy action-adventure film based on the first season of the animated television series, Avatar: The Last Airbender (2005–2008). Written and directed by M. Night Shyamalan , the film stars an ensemble cast featuring an array of young actors and is known for its ambitious story of elemental manipulation and heroism.
