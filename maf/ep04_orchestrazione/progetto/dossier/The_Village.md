# The Village (2004)
*Regia di M. Night Shyamalan*

- **Incasso**: $256.7 million
- **Rotten Tomatoes**: 43% (220 recensioni, media 5.4/10)
- **Metacritic**: 44/100 (40 critici)
- **CinemaScore**: C
- **Consenso della critica**: The Village is appropriately creepy, but Shyamalan's signature twist ending disappoints.

## Trama
The Village (marketed as M. Night Shyamalan's The Village) is a 2004 American period thriller film written, produced, and directed by M. Night Shyamalan. It stars Bryce Dallas Howard, Joaquin Phoenix, Adrien Brody, William Hurt, Sigourney Weaver, and Brendan Gleeson.

## Recensioni
- **[negativa]** Roger Ebert (Chicago Sun-Times): The Village is a colossal miscalculation, a movie based on a premise that cannot support it, a premise so transparent it would be laughable were the movie not so deadly solemn ... To call the ending an anticlimax would be an insult not only to climaxes but to prefixes. It's a crummy secret, about one step up the ladder of narrative originality from It was all a dream. It's so witless, in fact, that when we do discover the secret, we want to rewind the film so we don't know the secret anymore.
